The first stable release, and nine months of work since 0.1.17.

### Effects

Six new effects join Dynamics, Transform, Blur and Synthesise:

- **Attract** pulls energy onto a map of valleys, built from the scale, the grid, the source file or a modulator.
- **Transmute** turns one part of the sound into another: the level into the pitch, the phase into the pan, the position in the brush into the level.
- **Evolve** grows, spreads and decays the sound on its own, into fluid, unpredictable life.
- **Convolve** prints another sound's character onto this one: rooms, plates, resonant junk.
- **Binaural** places the sound around the listener's head: left, right, close, far, behind.
- **Sort** reorders the bands by loudness or phase, banking them into glitched stripes.

**Repeat** takes over from Overtones, copying the sound at beat and semitone offsets and snapping the copies to the scale. **Transform** gained a rate control, a chosen origin for scale and rotation, and a stretch that holds transients sharp.

### Brushes

- **Palettes** group brushes into named sets you open side by side, and the picker splits into Factory and User tabs.
- **Macros** sweep a set of parameters from one control, on every brush.
- **Presets** sit on every effect and modulator, and the modulators gained a sequencer and rates that follow the grid or the brush.
- **Fill Grid** paints the brush on every cell of the grid in one pass.

### Files and sound

- **Splitting** separates a file into drums, bass, vocals and other, into harmonic and percussive layers, or into any number of learned parts. The parts stay editable and merge back losslessly. Neural separation is not available on Intel Macs.
- **Onsets** are detected in every analysis, marked on the canvas, and snapped to by the grid and the brush.
- **Levels** are calibrated to dBFS, with a strip above each file and a limiter that holds down the stroke you paint rather than the file you export.
- **Mono and stereo** show in the file header and convert either way, and the analysis resolution is set from the badge.
- **Spectrogram image export** from the File menu.

### Around the app

- A bundled **manual**, a **walkthrough** and a **?** overlay that answers for any single control.
- **History** is a branching tree with lanes you can collapse, and undo and redo cost the size of the stroke rather than the size of the file.
- An **Ableton Live extension** ships beside the app.
- **Automatic updates**, from signed and notarised builds on macOS.
- Pan, zoom, painting and analysis stay responsive on long files.
