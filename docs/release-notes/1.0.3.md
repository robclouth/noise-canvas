A release about speed and memory on older machines, with the fixes found along the way.

### Faster

- **A small brush stays fast on a long file.** Painting used to slow down as the file got longer, whatever the size of the brush. Now the brush is what costs, not the file.
- **Hovering stays smooth**, because the preview under the cursor is far cheaper to draw.
- **Each open file uses much less graphics memory**, so longer files fit on machines that ran out before.
- **Macs that share memory with the graphics chip** are measured properly. The app used to read the whole machine's memory, assume too much was free, and run out.
- **Undo and redo take what the machine can spare** rather than a fixed amount.
- **The app does less while you do nothing.** It no longer checks the transport and the metronome many times a second.

### New

- **Show Log File**, in the Help menu, opens the folder holding the app's log. The log records what the app did, how much memory it used, and anything that went wrong. Send it with a bug report.
- **Drag a file's header** to reorder the stack of files on the canvas.
- **The app tells you when your graphics card cannot cope.** A card that cannot hold a spectrogram now says so, and running out of graphics memory mid-stroke says that, rather than failing in silence.

### Fixed

- **Each step builds on the one before it.** In a brush with more than one step, every step after the first threw away what the earlier steps had painted.
- **Some effects in a later step did nothing at all.**
- **Step Linked no longer wipes steps you had already set** when you switch it on from a step you added later.
- **Undo clears the whole stroke.** A brush whose later step reached further left some of the paint behind.

### Elsewhere

- The update dialogs match the rest of the app.
- The Beats and Semis controls drop their unit.
- Poster image export has wider margins, and the title stays clear on a Story crop.
